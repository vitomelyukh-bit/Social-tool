Data deletion instructions

Social Tool does not use Facebook Login and does not collect personal data of Facebook or Instagram users. It only stores data about the Pages, Instagram professional accounts and ad accounts of clients who granted us access.

  1. Email {% if owner.email %}{{ owner.email }}{% else %}{{ L.v(none) }}{% endif %} with the subject "Social Tool data deletion".
  2. Tell us the Facebook Page, Instagram account or ad account ID whose data should be deleted.
  3. Within 30 days we delete the client settings, scheduled posts and uploaded files, and confirm by email.

You can also revoke our access at any time in Meta Business Suite (Settings › Partners, or Users › System users). Published posts and campaigns remain in your Meta account, where you can delete them. Leads from lead forms never pass through our systems.
